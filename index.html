pods-rclone

pods-rclone is a small private tool. It copies simulation files (building models, plans, rendered images and videos) between the developer's own computers and a single folder in the developer's own Google Drive, using the open-source program rclone and the Google Drive API.

It has one user: the developer. It is not offered to the public, has no accounts and no sign-up, and no one else is asked to sign in with Google.
